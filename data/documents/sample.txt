Agroforestry systems in semi-arid regions have been shown to sequester 0.5-1.5 tC/ha/year in above-ground biomass and 0.2-0.4 tC/ha/year in soil organic carbon. Integrating trees into monoculture systems increases soil organic carbon by 10-20% and species richness by up to 30%. Legume cover crops fix nitrogen and increase soil organic carbon by approximately 15-25% over 2-3 years. Water harvesting structures such as check dams and contour trenches improve soil moisture by up to 40% in low-rainfall areas, supporting vegetation and increasing habitat for insects and birds. Riparian buffers of 30m width reduce sediment load by 70-90% and support 2-3x more aquatic macroinvertebrate taxa.
